Pro Tips & Controls

Essential tricks to get the most out of your lyrics.

🖐️

Moving the Window

Drag from the top-left grip handle (the LyricFlow logo). You can also use Shift+Arrows for micro-nudges.

👻

Click-Through Mode

Press Ctrl+Shift+L to make the window transparent to mouse clicks—perfect for gaming or work.

📋

Copy & Share

Double-click or press Ctrl+Shift+C to copy lyrics. Press Ctrl+Shift+S to share a card.

📌

Modes & Tray Control

Switch between Floating Overlay, Taskbar Dock, and Desktop Wallpaper from Settings or the system tray icon.
